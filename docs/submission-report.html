Product Development MVP Submission

Dhaba Prep Assistant

Google Antigravity AI-Assisted Product Build & Implementation Report

Executive One-Line Scope

My MVP lets owners of small independent food businesses (dhabas, cafes, small hotels) do a 3-minute daily log of what they made, what is left and what was wasted, plan tomorrow's prep from it, and count closing stock at shift change, so that they cook closer to real demand and notice missing stock at handover instead of absorbing the loss.

3. Mathematical Suggestion Engine

Exact Formula from Section 1.8:
1. Daily Sold = Made - Left - Wasted (min 0)
2. Average Sold = Sum(Sold of up to 7 recent days) / N
3. Suggested = Average Sold × (1 + 0.10 BUFFER), rounded to nearest 0.5 kg/L
4. Total to cook = Adjusted + Party Extras

4. Plain-Language Code Explanations (Oral Review Notes)

  • index.html: Holds the 5 visual screens (Today, EOD Log, Prep, Closing, Party Orders) and floating toast confirmation.
  • css/styles.css: Enforces 48px+ touch targets for oily/wet kitchen fingers and single terracotta dhaba palette.
  • js/firebase-config.js: Connection parameters for Google Cloud Firestore.
  • js/db.js: Centralized storage layer with automatic offline fallback adapter and Firestore cloud reads/writes.
  • js/suggestion.js: Implements the 10% safety buffer and 0.5 kg rounding algorithm.
  • js/app.js: Controls touch navigation, form validation (blocks negative numbers), and stepper buttons.
  • firestore.rules: Cloud security rules locking Firestore access strictly to the 6 application collections.
